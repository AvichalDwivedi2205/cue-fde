## Post text

Bringing FDE to consumers with Cue.

Cue brings customer reports from your widget, email, Slack and X into one inbox. It learns from your company’s guides and FAQs to answer questions with sources. Unanswered questions become FAQ drafts, and feature requests stay together so your team knows what people want.

When something breaks, Cue groups the reports, verifies the bug and sends it to a coding agent. The fix returns as a draft pull request with independent checks. Your team reviews it in Slack, approves the change, and Cue verifies the deployment before updating the customer.

Cue also finds people on X asking for something your product offers. It drafts replies in your brand’s voice for review and tracks whether visitors actually use the product after clicking.

@neatlogs helped us inspect agent traces, investigate errors and catch incorrect search logic. @EntireHQ connected our code changes to the agent conversations behind them. @arithecfo helped us work through pricing, costs and margins. LiteLLM gave us model choices for different agent tasks.
